# English only, with your Vocabulary as key terms

Every Capture is transcribed as English. The language is fixed rather than detected, because detection can guess wrong on short clips. Your Vocabulary is a table in the Inbox database, edited from a small Vocabulary screen in Jot, and it's sent as key terms on every call.

## Consequences

- The Vocabulary is capped at 100 terms until the vendor's real limit is verified.
- Prompting "Add 'X' to Vocabulary?" when you correct a word is a follow-up ticket, not part of v2.
- If you start dictating in other languages, revisit this.
